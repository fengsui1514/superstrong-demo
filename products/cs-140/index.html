What is the CS-140 used for?

The CS-140 is the high-output model in the range, intended for dense hardwood, engineered timber and crews that run a saw through long shifts. It shares the blade, bore and base with the CS-115.

How deep does the CS-140 cut at 90° and 45°?

68 mm at 90° and 47 mm at 45°, with a 0–45° bevel. Cutting depth is measured with the blade fitted and the base flat on the workpiece.

What is the difference between the CS-115 and CS-140?

Both are 185 mm circular saws on the same platform. The CS-115 runs a 1,800 W motor at 5,800 rpm with 65 mm depth at 90°; the CS-140 runs 2,000 W at 6,000 rpm with a revised gear set and 68 mm depth at 90°.

Is the CS-140 available for private label?

Yes. Housing colour, logo, rating plate, carton artwork and manual language can be specified for your market, and certification files are supplied with the order.

What voltage does the CS-140 use?

The sample build is 230 V / 50 Hz. Tell us the destination market and we will confirm the voltage and plug configuration for your order.

Can I order a sample before a production run?

Yes. Sample units can be shipped before a production commitment so your team can test the saw and approve packaging.
