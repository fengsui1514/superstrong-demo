What can a 185 mm circular saw cut?

A 185 mm saw is sized for framing timber, roof battens, decking boards, sheet goods and general site carpentry. With 65 mm maximum depth at 90°, the CS-115 handles standard framing lumber and most panel sizing. For repeated straight cuts in sheet goods, a track or table saw is a better fit.

How deep does the CS-115 cut at 90° and 45°?

65 mm at 90° and 45 mm at 45°, with a 0–45° bevel. Cutting depth is measured with the blade fitted and the base flat on the workpiece.

What blade does the CS-115 use?

A 185 mm blade with a 20 mm bore; a 24T carbide blade is fitted as standard. Any compatible 185 mm blade can be used, including finer-tooth blades for clean panel cuts.

Is the CS-115 available for private label?

Yes. It is the model we build most often for OEM and private-label programs. Housing colour, logo, rating plate, carton artwork and manual language can be specified, and certification files are supplied with the order.

What voltage and plug options are available?

The sample build is 230 V / 50 Hz. Tell us the destination market and we will confirm the voltage and plug configuration for your order.

Can I order a sample before a production run?

Yes. Sample units can be shipped before a production commitment so your team can test the saw and approve packaging.
