(01) Customisation

Built to your
market, not ours.

Our default model is a starting point. Every OEM program is specified around the destination market, the sales channel and the price point you need to hit.

Changes are quoted with the order so the cost is clear before tooling starts — no surprises when the artwork is approved.

  • Housing colour and finish matched to your brand or a Pantone reference.
  • Logo, rating plate, model number and serial label applied on the line.
  • Carton artwork, manual language and retail-ready packaging quoted with the order.
  • Blade, cable length and plug configuration specified for your market.
  • Certification files (CE / GS / RoHS) packed with the shipment.
  • Private-label or neutral packaging for distributor programs.
(02) The process

From spec sheet
to shipment.

One team covers the sample, the artwork and the production run. You approve each step before the next one starts.

01

Spec review

Send the model, market, voltage and plug, packaging brief and certification requirements. We reply with a quote and a written spec sheet.

02

Sample

A sample unit is built and shipped for testing. Packaging and artwork can be reviewed at the same time.

03

Artwork & tooling

Carton, manual and label files are proofed. Any new tooling or fixtures are quoted and approved before work starts.

04

Pilot run

A short run confirms the labels, packaging and finish before the full order is released.

05

Production & QC

The order runs on the same line as our own models, with 100% run-test and AQL final inspection before packing.

FAQ

Before you
ask.

Prefer to talk it through? Send your market and volume and we will reply with the next practical step.

Contact the export team →

What is the minimum order for a private-label circular saw?

MOQ depends on the model, branding and packaging scope. Send your target volume and we will confirm the minimum for your configuration.

Can you match our carton artwork and manual?

Yes. Carton artwork, manuals, labels and market-specific plugs are quoted with the order. We proof the files with you before production.

Which certifications are available?

The circular-saw platform is offered with CE, GS and RoHS documentation. Tell us the destination market and we will confirm which files apply.

Can we test a sample before committing to a production run?

Yes. Sample units can be shipped before a production commitment so your team can test the saw and approve the packaging.

Do you build cordless saws?

Our current platform is corded 185 mm circular saws. Other categories and cordless programs can be discussed as a development project.
