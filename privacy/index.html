Privacy policy

How Super Strong Power Tools handles personal data submitted through this website, and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 2026

1. Who we are

Super Strong Power Tools (“we”, “us”) manufactures circular saws and power tools in Ningbo, Zhejiang, China. For questions about this policy or your personal data, contact us at sales@example.com.

2. What we collect

We only collect information you choose to send us, plus limited technical data needed to run the site:

  • Inquiry details — name, email address, company, country, model of interest, estimated quantity and your message.
  • Technical data — standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages requested) kept by our hosting provider for security and reliability.
  • Analytics data — only if you accept analytics cookies: pages viewed, approximate location derived from IP, device and referral information.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your inquiry and prepare a quotation (our legitimate interest and, where required, steps taken at your request before entering a contract).
  • To maintain the security and performance of the website (legitimate interest).
  • To understand which pages are useful, if you consent to analytics cookies (consent).

4. Cookies and analytics

This site uses essential cookies only by default. If analytics is enabled, the Google Analytics 4 script is blocked until you click “Accept analytics”. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site’s cookies in your browser.

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site tracking pixels or sell personal data.

5. Form submissions

Inquiry forms are delivered by email or through a form-processing provider configured by us. That provider only processes the data to deliver the message and is bound by its own data-protection terms. We keep business correspondence for as long as needed to serve the commercial relationship and to meet legal or accounting obligations.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the website and respond to inquiries (hosting, email, analytics), or where required by law.

7. International transfers

We are based in China and may correspond with partners worldwide. When personal data is transferred internationally, we take reasonable steps to ensure it receives an appropriate level of protection.

8. Retention

Inquiry emails and quotations are retained for up to three years after the last contact, unless a longer period is required for a contract, warranty or legal obligation. Analytics data is retained according to the configured retention period (normally 14 months).

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • request access to the personal data we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • withdraw consent to analytics at any time;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, email sales@example.com. We will respond within one month where the applicable law requires it.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to inquiry data is limited to staff who need it to answer your request. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your data.

11. Changes

We may update this policy when our tools or legal obligations change. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
